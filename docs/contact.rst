Contact
=======

**dmokit** is developed by the Digital Medicine group at the Luxembourg Centre
for Systems Biomedicine (LCSB), University of Luxembourg.

The group
---------

The `Digital Medicine group <https://www.uni.lu/lcsb-en/research-groups/digital-medicine/>`_
at the LCSB develops and validates digital health technologies — wearable
sensors and digital biomarkers — to monitor neurological and movement disorders
such as Parkinson's disease in real-world conditions, connecting digital
mobility assessment to clinical outcomes and everyday patient care.
